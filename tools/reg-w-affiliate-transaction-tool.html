Regulation W — Affiliate Transaction Compliance Tool

A deterministic rules engine for Sections 23A & 23B of the Federal Reserve Act (12 CFR Part 223): single-affiliate and aggregate quantitative limits, collateral haircuts, the market-terms requirement, and an LOB-to-top-of-house exposure ledger. Fixed if/then logic — no model call anywhere in the determination path.
